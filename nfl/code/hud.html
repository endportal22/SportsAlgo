Power rankings

Power is points better than an average team on a neutral field, using the current model's weights (overall strength, pass and run game, projected QB). Offense and defense columns are EPA per play; lower is better on defense.

Players

Impact is points per game a player adds to his unit at full snaps, compared with an average player, estimated from how his offense or defense performs with him on the field. It's refit every week. Production (QB, RB, WR, TE) is his own EPA per dropback, carry or target in points per game. Linemen who always play together share credit, so treat their numbers as rough.

Backtest

Each season predicted using only earlier seasons for training. Log loss and Brier score measure the probabilities (lower is better). A coin flip scores 0.693 log loss.
